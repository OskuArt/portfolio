PRINTARI

I owned the brand's visual direction end to end: marketplace communication, market-led creative concepts, production standards and internal design operations.

Middle+/Senior DesignerVisual ownershipE-commerceDesign systems

What I owned

My role went beyond producing layouts. I was responsible for how the brand looked, how new visual ideas were generated and how the design workflow itself could become more structured.

01 · Art direction

Brand visual ownership

Maintained and developed the visual language across marketplace cards, promotional graphics, product presentation and print materials, keeping the brand recognizable while pushing stronger new concepts.

02 · Market analysis

Ideas backed by research

Reviewed competitors, category conventions and marketplace patterns before proposing new visual directions. The goal was not decoration for decoration's sake, but clearer product value and stronger shelf differentiation.

03 · Design operations

A clearer team workflow

Initiated a more structured approach to designer work: reusable logic for assets, clearer organization, tagging and a shared system for finding, checking and maintaining a growing print library.

Print catalog system

My largest internal project: a browser-based tool for working with a large print library, designed to replace manual searching and duplicate checking with a much clearer visual workflow.

Internal tool · UX + logic + automation

One place for every print.

I proposed and developed the structure of a dedicated print-management website. It combines previews, metadata and filters with an automated similarity layer, so designers can spot repeated graphics before preparing another version of the same asset.

Market-led visuals

Three visual directions developed from marketplace research. Each system solves a different communication task while staying inside one brand ecosystem.

Direction 01

Bright everyday utility

7 visualsfeature-first hierarchy

A cleaner and brighter system for fast product reading. The composition keeps the case central while lifestyle props, close-ups and benefit callouts make functionality easier to scan on a marketplace feed.

Direction 02

Universal passport cover system

8 visualsmulti-country compatibility message

A blue travel-led direction for passport covers, balancing product protection, print quality and universal fit. The system was designed to communicate compatibility quickly without turning the card into a wall of copy.

Direction 03

Travel lifestyle narrative

8 visualsbenefit-led storytelling

A warmer lifestyle story using luggage, airport and travel cues. It connects the passport cover to the situation where it is actually used, while keeping the product clearly visible and commercially readable.
